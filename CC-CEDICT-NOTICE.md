# CC-CEDICT (Chinese–English dictionary)

The optional Chinese-to-English database is generated locally by [`Setup-Dictionary.ps1`](Setup-Dictionary.ps1) from [CC-CEDICT, published by MDBG](https://www.mdbg.net/chinese/dictionary?page=cc-cedict). Credit goes to the CC-CEDICT contributors and to Paul Andrew Denisowski for the original CEDICT (copyright 1997, 1998). See the [CC-CEDICT project](https://cc-cedict.org/) and its [CC BY-SA 4.0 license](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/).

The local import indexes both simplified and traditional forms, keeps up to two distinct English senses per headword and omits classifier notes. The database records the source URL, downloaded file's SHA-256, attribution and license. The downloaded source and derived database are not distributed with this repository. The adapted dictionary data remains subject to CC BY-SA 4.0.
